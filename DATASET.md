## 2. Scheme Details & Grounding

### Central Schemes (6)

1. **PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)**
   - **Authority**: Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
   - **Eligibility**: All landholding farmer families with cultivable landholding in their names. Excludes institutional landholders, constitutional post holders, serving/retired government employees, and income tax payers.
   - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar Card, Bank Account / Passbook, Landholding / Domicile records.
   - **Application**: Online at `https://pmkisan.gov.in/` under 'New Farmer Registration' or through Common Service Centres (CSC).

2. **Ayushman Bharat – Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)**
   - **Authority**: National Health Authority, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare
   - **Eligibility**: Families identified through SECC 2011 deprivation criteria; expanded universal coverage for all senior citizens aged 70+.
   - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar Card, Ration Card.
   - **Application**: Check eligibility at `https://nha.gov.in/PM-JAY` and generate Ayushman Card at empanelled hospitals.

3. **Pradhan Mantri Jan-Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)**
   - **Authority**: Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance
   - **Eligibility**: Any Indian citizen aged 10+ without a basic savings account.
   - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar Card, Official ID.
   - **Application**: In-person at any commercial bank, Regional Rural Bank (RRB), or Bank Mitra outlet.

4. **Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)**
   - **Authority**: Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance
   - **Eligibility**: Indian citizens aged 18–50 with a bank/post office savings account and auto-debit consent.
   - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar Card, Bank Account details.
   - **Fee**: ₹436/year premium (official application/premium fee exists).
   - **Application**: Participating bank branches, post offices, or internet banking.

5. **Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)**
   - **Authority**: Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance
   - **Eligibility**: Indian citizens aged 18–70 with an active savings account and auto-debit consent.
   - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar Card, Bank Account details.
   - **Fee**: ₹20/year premium (official application/premium fee exists).
   - **Application**: Participating bank branches, post offices, or internet banking.

6. **PM Vishwakarma**
   - **Authority**: Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises
   - **Eligibility**: Traditional artisans and craftspeople aged 18+ working in the 18 notified family trades with hands and tools.
   - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar Card, Bank Account details, Official ID.
   - **Application**: CSC registration on `https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/` with three-stage verification.

### Uttar Pradesh Schemes (14)

7. **Mukhyamantri Kanya Sumangala Yojana**
   - **Authority**: Women and Child Development Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh
   - **Eligibility**: Resident girl child of UP. Family income under ₹3,00,000/year. Up to 2 girl children per family.
   - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar, Bank Passbook, Income Certificate, Residence Proof, Birth Certificate.
   - **Application**: Online via `https://mksy.up.gov.in/`.

8. **UP Vridhavastha Pension Yojana (Old Age Pension)**
   - **Authority**: Social Welfare Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh
   - **Eligibility**: UP resident aged 60+ living below poverty line (rural income ≤ ₹46,080; urban income ≤ ₹56,460).
   - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar, Bank Passbook, Income Certificate, Residence Proof.
   - **Application**: Online via `https://sspy-up.gov.in/`.

9. **UP Nirashrit Mahila Pension Yojana (Destitute Widow Pension)**
   - **Authority**: Women Welfare Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh
   - **Eligibility**: Destitute widow aged 18+ resident of UP. Annual income ≤ ₹2,00,000. Not remarried.
   - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar, Bank Passbook, Income Certificate, Residence Proof, Husband's Death Certificate.
   - **Application**: Online via `https://sspy-up.gov.in/`.

10. **UP Divyangjan Pension Yojana (Disability Pension)**
    - **Authority**: Divyangjan Empowerment Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh
    - **Eligibility**: UP resident aged 18+ with minimum 40% certified disability. Income ≤ ₹46,080 (rural) / ₹56,460 (urban).
    - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar, Bank Passbook, Income Certificate, CMO Disability Certificate, Residence Proof.
    - **Application**: Online via `https://sspy-up.gov.in/`.

11. **UP Shadi Anudan Yojana (Marriage Assistance)**
    - **Authority**: Social Welfare Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh
    - **Eligibility**: BPL UP resident families. Bride age ≥ 18, groom age ≥ 21. Rural income ≤ ₹46,080 / urban ≤ ₹56,460.
    - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar, Bank Details, Income Certificate, Caste Certificate, Residence Proof, Wedding Card/Form.
    - **Application**: Online via `https://shadianudan.upsdc.gov.in/` within 90 days before/after marriage.

12. **UP Post-Matric Scholarship and Fee Reimbursement Scheme**
    - **Authority**: Social Welfare / Backward Class Welfare Departments, Government of Uttar Pradesh
    - **Eligibility**: UP domicile studying in recognized post-matric programs. Income ≤ ₹2.5L (SC/ST) or ≤ ₹2.0L (Gen/OBC/Minority).
    - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar, Bank Account (NPCI mapped), Income Certificate, Caste Certificate, Marksheets, Fee Receipt.
    - **Application**: Online via `https://scholarship.up.gov.in/`.

13. **UP Gopalak Yojana**
    - **Authority**: Animal Husbandry Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh
    - **Eligibility**: UP resident aged 18+ with dairy experience and cattle shed land. Minimum 5 milch cattle initially. Income ≤ ₹1,00,000.
    - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar, Bank Passbook, Income Certificate, Residence Proof, Proposal.
    - **Application**: Application to Chief Veterinary Officer (CVO) / District Animal Husbandry Office.

14. **UP Mukhyamantri Krishak Durghatna Kalyan Yojana**
    - **Authority**: Board of Revenue, Government of Uttar Pradesh
    - **Eligibility**: Farmers, account-holders, or agricultural laborers in UP aged 18–70.
    - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar, Bank Account, Land/Tenancy Records, Medical/Post-Mortem Report.
    - **Application**: Tehsil Office / SDM within 45 days of accident.

15. **Mukhyamantri Abhyudaya Yojana**
    - **Authority**: Social Welfare Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh
    - **Eligibility**: Youth resident in UP preparing for competitive exams (UPSC, UPPSC, JEE, NEET, NDA, CDS).
    - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar, Residence Proof, Education Marksheets.
    - **Application**: Online registration at `https://abhyuday.up.gov.in/` followed by screening test.

16. **Swami Vivekanand Yuva Sashaktikaran Yojana (DigiShakti)**
    - **Authority**: Department of IT and Electronics, Government of Uttar Pradesh
    - **Eligibility**: Regular students enrolled in higher/technical education in UP. Income ≤ ₹2,00,000.
    - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar, College ID, Enrolment Certificate.
    - **Application**: Direct institutional enrolment upload onto `https://digishakti.up.gov.in/`.

17. **UP Mukhyamantri Bal Seva Yojana**
    - **Authority**: Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Uttar Pradesh
    - **Eligibility**: Orphaned children (0–18 years) resident in UP who lost parents/guardians. Income ≤ ₹3,00,000 (waived for COVID-19).
    - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar, Bank Passbook, Parents' Death Certificate, Guardianship Order.
    - **Application**: District Probation Officer (DPO) / Child Welfare Committee (CWC).

18. **UP Vishwakarma Shram Samman Yojana**
    - **Authority**: Directorate of Industries and Enterprise Promotion, Government of Uttar Pradesh
    - **Eligibility**: Traditional artisans (tailors, barbers, carpenters, potters, blacksmiths) in UP aged 18+.
    - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar, Bank Details, Residence Proof, Trade Proof.
    - **Application**: Online via `https://diupmsme.upsdc.gov.in/` and interview at DIC.

19. **One District One Product (ODOP) Margin Money Scheme**
    - **Authority**: Department of MSME & Export Promotion, Government of Uttar Pradesh
    - **Eligibility**: UP resident aged 18+ with minimum Class 8 education setting up unit in district's notified ODOP craft.
    - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar, Bank Details, Residence Proof, Class 8+ Certificate, DPR (Detailed Project Report).
    - **Application**: Online via `https://odopup.in/` / `https://diupmsme.upsdc.gov.in/`.

20. **UP Mukhyamantri Gramodyog Rozgar Yojana**
    - **Authority**: UP Khadi and Village Industries Board (UPKVIB), Government of Uttar Pradesh
    - **Eligibility**: Rural UP residents aged 18–50 establishing village enterprises. Class 8 pass or ITI certification.
    - **Required Documents**: Aadhaar, Bank Passbook, Income Certificate, Caste Certificate, Project Proposal.
    - **Application**: Online via `https://upkvib.gov.in/` and district lead bank sanction.

## 3. Ingestion and Qdrant RAG Architecture

### Step 1: PostgreSQL Seeding (`SchemeDataInitializer`)
When the Spring Boot application boots, `SchemeDataInitializer` executes:
1. Creates document types (`AADHAAR`, `BANK_ACCOUNT`, `INCOME_CERTIFICATE`, `DISABILITY_CERTIFICATE`, etc.).
2. Idempotently inserts or updates the 20 schemes (`findByName(...)` check).
3. Attaches structured eligibility rules (`STATE = Uttar Pradesh`, `MIN_AGE`, `MAX_AGE`, `MAX_INCOME`, `OCCUPATION`, `GENDER`, `DISABILITY_STATUS`).
4. Attaches document requirements and official application steps.
5. Populates transparency metadata (official fees, verified application channels, official grievance portals: `pgportal.gov.in` for Central and `jansunwai.up.gov.in` for Uttar Pradesh).

### Step 2: Content Chunking (`SchemeChunkingService`)
For each of the 20 schemes, 6 specialized document chunks are prepared:
- `ELIGIBILITY_EXPLANATION`: Plain-text criteria, detailed rules, and official verification source.
- `BENEFITS`: Verified monetary and non-monetary benefits.
- `REQUIRED_DOCUMENTS`: Mandatory and optional documents with codes and notes.
- `APPLICATION_PROCESS`: Step-by-step guidance and portal links.
- `IMPORTANT_CONDITIONS`: Exclusions, prerequisites, and eligibility limits.
- `FAQS_OR_RAW_TEXT`: Reference document links and FAQs.

Total chunks: **20 schemes × 6 chunks = 120 document chunks**.

### Step 3: Embeddings & Vector Storage (`SchemeVectorIndexingService`)
1. Generates deterministic UUID point IDs based on `UUID.nameUUIDFromBytes(schemeId + ":" + chunkType)`.
2. Stores relational chunks in PostgreSQL `scheme_document_chunks`.
3. Pushes documents to Spring AI `VectorStore` (Qdrant collection `swatva_schemes`) with metadata:
   - `schemeId`
   - `schemeName`
   - `governmentLevel`
   - `state`
   - `category`
   - `documentType`
   - `sourceUrl`
4. Idempotency guarantee: Re-indexing uses deterministic point IDs, upserting existing vectors in Qdrant and preventing duplicates.

### Step 4: Semantic Search & RAG (`SchemeRagService` & `SchemeRetrievalService`)
- Semantic similarity search: `vectorStore.similaritySearch(request)` matches user query with top chunks.
- Database keyword search: Fallback if vector database is empty or offline.
- RAG generation: Answers citizen questions using **only** retrieved context; explicitly returns `"The system does not have enough verified information to answer this question. Please refer to the official government portals."` if context is insufficient.

## 4. Triggering Indexing Manually

You can trigger vector indexing via the REST API:

```powershell
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Post -Uri "http://localhost:8080/api/ai/index"
```

Response:
```json
{
  "success": true,
  "data": {
    "schemesIndexed": 20,
    "chunksIndexed": 120,
    "message": "Indexed 20 schemes with 120 document chunks."
  },
  "timestamp": "2026-09-27T04:15:00Z"
}
```

Or test semantic queries:
```powershell
$body = @{ query = "scholarship for higher education in Uttar Pradesh"; state = "Uttar Pradesh" } | ConvertTo-Json
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Post -Uri "http://localhost:8080/api/ai/scheme-query" -ContentType "application/json" -Body $body
```
